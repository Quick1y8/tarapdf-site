TaraPDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026 · Türkçe

TaraPDF (“the app”) is a document scanner, PDF editor and QR code reader. This page explains what information is processed when you use it. In short: your documents, photos and records stay on your phone. The only exception is the fast background removal used by Plus and Pro members (section 2).

1. No account

You don’t need an account, name, email address or phone number to use the app. We have no user database; our only server relays Plus/Pro fast background removal and stores nothing.

2. Your documents and photos

  • Documents you scan, photos you pick, PDFs, your signature, expense records and reminders are stored only in the app’s folder on your phone.
  • Text recognition (OCR), face detection and table reading run on your phone. This content is never uploaded to any server.
  • On the Free plan, background removal runs on your phone. Plus and Pro members can use fast mode: only the photo whose background you want removed is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to the TaraPDF server and from there to our image-processing partner; the result with a transparent background is sent back to your phone. The photo is used only for this operation, is not stored and is not processed for any other purpose. To verify your membership, the Google Play purchase token is also sent; no name, email or location is sent. The partner’s servers may be located outside your country.
  • When you share a file (e.g. via WhatsApp or email), it goes only to the app you choose.
  • Uninstalling the app or using Settings → “Delete all data” removes all of this data from your phone.

3. Ads (Free plan only)

On the Free plan, ads are shown through Google AdMob. Plus and Pro have no ads and the ad system is never started.

  • To show ads, Google may collect information such as the advertising ID, device model, operating system, language, approximate location (from the IP address) and ad interactions.
  • The ad system cannot access your documents, photos or records; they are never sent to the ad network.
  • Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent; you can change it in Settings → “Ad privacy options”.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are made through Google Play. Google processes payment details; we never see your card or payment information. The app only stores which plan is active, on your phone.

5. Google components

Google ML Kit is used for the document scanner, text recognition and barcode reading. These components work on your phone; Google may collect anonymous usage and diagnostics (e.g. processing time, device model) to improve the service, but scanned images and recognized text are not sent.

6. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents, QR codes and business cards.
  • Notifications: for document expiry reminders and the trial-ending reminder.
  • Internet: for ads on the Free plan, Google Play purchases and Plus/Pro fast background removal. Your documents are not sent over the internet.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick are read; only results you choose to save are written to your gallery.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Your rights

Because we don’t store your personal data on our side, requests such as deletion or correction can usually be fulfilled directly by you by deleting the data on your phone. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Mexico’s LFPDPPP, India’s DPDP Act or Turkey’s KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing; contact us for any request. For ad-related data you can also use Google’s privacy tools.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page with a new date.
